# Curvenote Platform

The Curvenote **Platform** is the hosted product. You sign in with an
SCM account (GitHub, GitLab, ORCID, Google, Okta), link a repository,
and Curvenote builds a site, versions your works, and runs checks on
every push. Journals can also accept submissions through the same
pipeline.

:::{note} Looking for the Editor?
The standalone Curvenote Editor (the in-browser writing app, formerly
documented as the "Write" docs) lives at
[curvenote.com/docs/write](https://curvenote.com/docs/write).
:::
